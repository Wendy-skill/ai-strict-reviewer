## Pre-submission Report

Separate all findings into:

### A. Must address before submission

Issues that materially affect scientific validity, methodological defensibility,
evidence supporting a major conclusion, original contribution, or examination/
review defensibility.

### B. Strongly recommended

Important improvements that strengthen the work but do not invalidate its central contribution.

### C. Optional improvement

Useful but not necessary for scientific defensibility.

### D. No change recommended

Issues considered during review where the existing treatment is already adequate.

For A or B items, provide:

- location
- problem
- why it matters
- required action
- whether new analysis/data is required

Do not generate new findings during consolidation.
